The **Canadian Digital Commons Institute (CDCI)** is a public-interest initiative dedicated to strengthening Canada's digital commons through open standards, open-source software, research, education, datasets, and digital infrastructure.

We support projects that improve the accessibility, interoperability, transparency, and long-term stewardship of digital knowledge and technology. Our work spans AI, digital governance, open documentation, provenance, public datasets, developer resources, and collaborative infrastructure designed to benefit Canadians and the global open ecosystem.

## Areas of Focus

- Open standards and specifications
- Open-source software
- Artificial intelligence and responsible AI
- Digital infrastructure
- Public datasets
- Research and technical publications
- Education and learning resources
- Documentation and maintainership
- Digital stewardship and governance

## Projects

- OSSI
- ProvenanceDB
- Canada AI
- Code the North
- Awesome Learn
- Awesome Lists

**Mission:** Build and steward open digital infrastructure that strengthens Canada's digital commons for generations to come.
